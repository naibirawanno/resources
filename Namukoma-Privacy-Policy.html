The short version

Namukoma works without an internet connection. You do not need to sign up or log in. NAIBIRA does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through the game. Your scores, stars, and unlocked levels are stored locally on your device.

01. About this policy

Namukoma (“the App”) is a mathematics game developed and published by NAIBIRA (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how the App handles information when you play it.

02. Information we collect

We do not collect personal or sensitive user data through Namukoma. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photographs, or other identifying information. It does not use advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics services.

There is no user registration, login, or online player profile. The App does not send gameplay information to NAIBIRA servers.

03. Game progress stored on your device

To make the game enjoyable across sessions, Namukoma saves gameplay progress on your device. This may include completed and unlocked levels, earned stars, and game settings. These records are used only to restore your progress and personalize the gameplay experience locally.

NAIBIRA does not receive or access this locally stored progress through the App. Depending on your device's operating-system settings, device-level backup services may handle app data independently of NAIBIRA.

04. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties through the App. Namukoma does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

If you choose to contact us by email, your message and email address will be handled by your email provider and ours to respond to your inquiry. This is separate from gameplay; we do not use support emails for advertising.

05. Device permissions and internet access

Namukoma is designed for fully offline play. It does not need an internet connection or request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or device media. It does not display runtime permission prompts for gameplay.

Operating-system functionality, such as opening an email application when you select a contact link, is controlled by your device and is not required to play.

06. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored progress remains on your device until you erase it using the App's reset-progress feature, clear the App's storage in device settings, or uninstall the App. Device backups, if enabled by the operating system, may have their own retention and restoration behavior.

Because we do not collect or maintain gameplay accounts or cloud gameplay records, NAIBIRA has no server-side gameplay data to delete. If you email us, you may request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

07. Age suitability

Namukoma is designed for individuals aged 13 and above. It offers mathematics challenges and does not require players to disclose their age or personal information. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

08. Security and privacy

Our primary privacy safeguard is data minimization: the App operates locally and does not transmit gameplay data to us. Locally stored game progress is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. We encourage users to keep their devices updated and use available device security features.

09. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App's functionality or applicable requirements change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. If a future version introduces data collection, we will update the disclosures before that functionality is released and provide any notices or choices required by law.
